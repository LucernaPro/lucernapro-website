How to use

Everything on this page, every number — the whole job hinges on one step: the 2–5 minute flash time. Do not miss it

  1. Prep: wash clean — machine polish recommended

    Wash off all dirt and oils — a machine polish beforehand is recommended; it improves both adhesion and the final gloss

    Clean surfacePolish first = better finish

  2. Coat one small panel at a time — never the whole car at once

    Put 2–3 drops on an applicator foam and spread across the panel, top to bottom, left to right, for even coverage — one section at a time only, because the flash window is short

    2–3 drops per panelOne panel at a time

  3. Flash time: wait 2 minutes, never past 5

    The most critical step of the job — wait at least 2 minutes but no more than 5, then gently wipe the excess with a microfibre cloth, buffing in circles until the surface gleams. Leave no residue or you'll get streaking / past the window = extremely hard to buff out

    2–5 min onlyMicrofibreZero residue

  4. Initial cure: 24 hours, no water

    Let it cure at least 24 hours somewhere dry — don't park it in the open, and keep it away from water for the full first 24 hours

    24 hrs, no water

  5. Full cure: 7 days — full properties

    The layer reaches full cure in 7 days, and only then delivers its complete hardness and hydrophobic performance

    Full system in 7 days
