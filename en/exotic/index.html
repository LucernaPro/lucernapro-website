1COMPONENT · READY FROM THE CAN

Ordinary waterproofing under new tilework — especially paired with ordinary grout — is a risk hiding under a pretty floor. Standard grout erodes easily by nature; once it starts to fail, water seeps down and sits trapped under the tiles in silence. If the waterproofing layer below can't survive ponding water, it slowly rots until nothing is left. The day a stain appears on the ceiling downstairs is the day the bomb has already gone off.

Exotic sits exactly at this pressure point — under-tile waterproofing that survives ponding water for a year. And because it's one component, there's no worrying whether the crew mixed it properly before the tiles went down. It cures every time, even with a careless contractor.

Already leaking — the decisive fix

Rake out the old grout and replace it with a dedicated waterproof grout — solves it for good, but it's the harder road.

Already leaking — the easier fix

Refill the grout lines with ordinary grout, then coat over the tile face with our purpose-made TileCoat Polyurea.

Building new — prevent it upfront

Don't wait for the problem. Choose a system that survives ponding water for a year from day one — Exotic is the top of the ready-to-use line, and if you want the absolute state of the art we make PMMA.

How to use

Genuinely DIY-able, all 6 steps — every number you need is on this page.

  1. Prep the surface

    The surface must be completely dry, free of dust, oil and contaminants — some surfaces may need a primer first. Not sure if yours does? Ask us on Messenger.

  2. Wide joints or deep gaps — always fill first

    If a joint has a real gap, fill it flush first with our SpackleFlex (or silicone), then coat over with Exotic — skip this and joint movement can tear the film.

    The combo: SpackleFlex fill → Exotic over

  3. Stir it properly

    One component still means stir thoroughly in the can before use. Then pour out only what you need and reseal the lid immediately.

    Never leave the lid open

  4. First coat

    Roller, brush or spray gear — cover the whole area evenly. An even film beats thick patches every time.

    Recommended 10–20 mils per coat

  5. Second coat

    Apply at least two coats for full system strength, about 2 hours after the first coat or once it has fully dried.

    ≈ 2 hours between coats

  6. Let it cure, then reopen the area

    Touch-dry in 1–2 hours, put the area back in service after 6 hours or more, and fully cured within 24 hours at 25°C.

    Touch-dry 1–2 hrsBack in service 6 hrs+Full cure 24 hrs
⚠ Caution (the one place you can slip up): Exotic dries extremely fast — while working, never leave the can open. Pour out only what you need, and once opened, plan to use the whole can in one session.
Straight talk on surfaces: Exotic bonds to almost everything, but it's not suited to painting directly onto glossy glazed tiles — for waterproofing over tiles we recommend PolyPro (two-part, higher performance on difficult surfaces) or TileCoat Polyurea, born for tiles specifically.
