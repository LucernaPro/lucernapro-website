Great value — but know these 3 things before ordering

5 SQ.M.per kit — measure your area before ordering

This kit is portioned to cover exactly 5 sq.m. — base coat, chips and clear coat. Measure your actual area first; for larger floors order more kits, or message us and we’ll calculate it for you.

Broadcast chips only while the coat is wet

The most critical timing in the whole job — as soon as a zone is coated, broadcast chips immediately while it’s still wet. Wait until it starts drying and the chips won’t stick.

Base coat pot life: 15 minutes

Once mixed, Marine Guard must be applied within 15 minutes. For larger areas, mix in batches — never the whole can at once.

Give it full cure time

Light foot traffic is fine 24 hours after the clear coat, but full hardness takes 5–7 days — no heavy scrubbing or dragging furniture in the first week.

How to do it step by step, done in 2 days

Every number you need is on this page — watch the video first, then follow the steps in order.

  1. Prep the surface — clean, minimal dust

    Wash the floor, remove grease and dust. Concrete can be damp but must have no standing water. For existing tile, scrub off all soap residue and wipe dry.

    Damp OK — no standing water

  2. Mix the Marine Guard base — A 2 parts : B 1 part (by weight)

    Always weigh with a scale. Stir thoroughly for at least 1 minute — once mixed you have 15 minutes to apply. Mix in batches for larger areas.

    A : B = 2 : 1Stir ≥ 1 minPot life 15 min

  3. Roll on the base coat evenly

    Use the roller from the kit and coat evenly, zone by zone — the kit is portioned for exactly 5 sq.m.

    Roller includedWork zone by zone

  4. Broadcast the Flake chips immediately while wet

    Scatter from about waist height for even coverage. Coat a zone, broadcast that zone — don’t wait for the coat to start drying or the chips won’t bond.

    Broadcast while wet only

  5. Wait ~6 hours, then sweep off loose chips

    Once the base coat is dry, sweep or vacuum off every chip that didn’t bond before applying the clear coat.

    Wait ~6 hrsRemove loose chips

  6. Mix the CrystalSeal clear coat — A 100 : B 14 (by weight)

    Weigh to ratio, then stir slowly for 2–3 minutes. This one has an hours-long pot life — no rush.

    A : B = 100 : 14Stir slowly 2–3 min

  7. Clear-coat the entire floor

    Roll CrystalSeal over all the chips. This layer locks the chips in, resists stains and seals out water at the surface.

    Always finish with clear coat

  8. Let it dry, then it’s ready

    Touch-dry in 1–2 hours, light use after 24 hours, full cure in 5–7 days.

    Light use 24 hrsFull cure 5–7 days
⚠ Most common mistakes: mixing the whole can of base coat at once and missing the 15-minute window, and broadcasting chips too late onto a drying coat. Work zone by zone — coat, then broadcast immediately — and every square meter comes out tight and beautiful.
