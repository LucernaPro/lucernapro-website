How to use

The only coat in our lineup you apply without racing the clock — pot life in hours · Two rules: grout must be full before you coat, and the ratio gets weighed, not eyeballed

  1. Prep the surface

    Scrub off soap scum, grease, dust and algae, then let everything dry completely — never apply over wet surfaces or standing water.

  2. Fill every grout line (do not skip)

    This step decides whether the job works. Any grout that's chipped, missing or grooved must be filled flush first. Coating over broken grout = a failed job; water will still find its way down. (If your old grout is far gone, our Epoxy Waterproof Grout will do nicely.)

  3. Weigh and mix — and stir like you mean it

    This one's ratio is special: weigh both parts on a digital scale — never eyeball it. Then stir slowly until genuinely uniform, scraping the bottom and sides of the bucket. A lazy stir means the coating never dries, and that's very hard to fix. Never use one part alone, and never add water or anything else. The upside: no rushing — once mixed, you have plenty of time to apply.

    Weigh Part A : Part B = 100 : 14Stir slowly 2–3 minutesPot life measured in hours

  4. First coat

    Roll on a thin, even film across the whole area — or for grout-lines-only protection, apply with a brush along the lines. (On outdoor wood, test a small patch first.)

  5. Second coat

    This one dries slower than its big brother — be patient and let the first coat set properly before recoating. Two coats is the minimum standard for a waterproofing system.

    Wait at least 4 hours between coats

  6. Want double the durability? Go to 4 coats

    Optional but worth it: build up to 3–4 coats for a thicker film and double the durability — ideal for balconies that take heavy sun and rain. (Same waiting time between every coat.)

    4 coats = double durability

  7. Let it cure, then enjoy

    After the final coat, let the film cure — this one dries slowly, but it rewards the wait. Every tile pattern exactly where you left it.

    Touch-dry 1–2 hoursLight use after 24 hoursFull cure 5–7 days
⚠ Caution: This one has a strong smell — we're telling you straight before you buy. Wear an odor-protection mask and always work in a well-ventilated space. Not suitable for people allergic to thinner or chemicals (if allergic, have a contractor apply it). If it splashes into eyes, rinse immediately with clean water and see a doctor.
