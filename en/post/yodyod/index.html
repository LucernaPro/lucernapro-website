"Yod Yod Explains" — Home Truths, One Drop at a Time, from Our Tiny Water-Drop Mascot

Updated whenever a new episode lands · by the LucernaPro team

Meet Yod Yod — a water drop in a yellow hard hat, and the mascot of LucernaPro. His job is simple: tell homeowners the home truths nobody in the trade ever tells them, in clips under half a minute. Watch one and you'll know exactly what breaks, why it breaks, and how to prevent it before it gets expensive.

Why a water drop? Because water is the secret main character behind nearly every kind of damage in a house — who better to tell these stories than the water itself. This page collects every episode in one place; new ones get added here.

EP.1Grout Is a Sponge

The bathroom leaks, yet not a single tile is cracked — because water doesn't go through tiles. It goes through the grout lines, whose insides are porous like a sponge. The first episode shows the mechanism with your own eyes.

Read the full story behind this episode: Why bathroom grout leaks keep coming back

EP.2Rusted Gutters Aren't Fate

Two gutters, installed the same day — only one rusted through. Because gutter rot isn't luck; it's the choice between leaving bare metal exposed and coating it from day one. This episode shows where rust starts and why the golden window is while the gutter is still new.

Read the full story behind this episode: Rusted gutters aren't fate — they're a choice
