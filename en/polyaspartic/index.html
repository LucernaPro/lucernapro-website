We tuned this formula for two things above all: adhesion on glazed tiles and resistance to standing water — and engineering like that always has a price. The trade-off is UV endurance on outdoor tiled floors. For outdoor work on surfaces that aren't tiled floors — steel, wood, fiberglass, marine jobs — this clear Polyaspartic handles it with ease and clearly outlasts ordinary PU. That's exactly why industry moved to this technology.

How to use

The easiest mix in two-part waterproofing — pour equal parts 1 : 1, stir, paint · 3–4 hours hands-on for the whole job · The one rule you can't skip: grout must be full before you coat

  1. Prep the surface

    Scrub off soap scum, grease and algae, then let everything dry completely — a clean surface is half the battle against peeling.

  2. Fill every grout line (do not skip)

    This step decides whether the job works. Any grout that's chipped, missing or grooved must be filled flush first. Coating over broken grout = a failed job; water will still find its way down. (If your old grout is far gone, our Epoxy Waterproof Grout will do nicely.)

  3. Mix Part A : Part B — and stir like you mean it

    Pour equal parts, then stir until genuinely uniform — scrape the bottom and sides of the bucket too. This is where jobs fail most often: a lazy stir means the coating never dries, and that's very hard to fix. Don't rush this step. Never use Part A on its own, and only mix what you can apply in time — it starts setting in the bucket.

    Mix 1 : 1Use within 15–20 minutes of mixing

  4. First coat

    Apply a thin, even film across the whole area, including every grout line — thin and even beats thick and patchy every time.

  5. Second coat

    Wait until the first coat is touch-dry, then coat again — two coats give the film the thickness a waterproofing system needs. 1 kg covers 5 m² with both coats.

    First coat touch-dry ~2 hoursRecoat within 6 hours

  6. Let it cure, then enjoy

    After the second coat, let the film cure before use or water contact — the longer the better. Then use your bathroom as normal, with every tile pattern exactly where you left it.

    Wait 4–6+ hours before water contact
⚠ Caution: Not suitable for people allergic to thinner or chemical products — if you have such allergies, don't apply it yourself; have a contractor do it. Always work in a well-ventilated space.
