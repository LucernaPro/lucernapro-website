STRAIGHT TALK — the part most sellers skip

Concentrated products demand technique —
one iron rule: apply thin. Thinner is better.

1×THIN COAT ONLY

PlastiBright is concentrated. A single thin film is the correct film — laying it on thick doesn’t make it blacker or tougher, it just gives you streaks, waste, and an ugly cure. If silicone dressings taught you to slather product on, unlearn that habit first.

And before you hit order, two things we’d rather tell you straight than let you find out the hard way:

The iron rule

Thin coats only — never apply thick. A few drops on the applicator, work one panel at a time, buff the excess off immediately. The thinner the coat, the deeper and more even the black.

Exterior only

The formula contains solvent to drive that deep penetration. It’s not that interior use is impossible — it’s that dashboards, buttons and screens are too expensive to gamble with. So we draw the line for you: exterior plastic only.

Painted plastic = no visible change

This product shines on raw black plastic that’s gone gray — bumpers, trim, grilles, mirror housings. Plastic that’s been painted can be treated, but you won’t see a difference. Don’t waste your money on those parts.

How to use

Everything you need is on this page — no hard steps, just two things that matter: a clean, bone-dry surface and thin coats only.

  1. Wash and dry completely

    The plastic must be clean — no dust, oil, or old wax residue — and fully dry before you start. The product only penetrates as deep as the surface lets it.

  2. Apply a thin, even coat

    A few drops on a sponge or applicator pad — just damp, not soaked — then work panel by panel. One thin pass is enough. Never lay it on thick, never slather. Thinner = deeper, more even black.

    One thin coat15–30ml per car

  3. Buff off the excess right away

    Gently wipe off any residue with a microfiber towel before it sets — don’t let streaks dry onto the surface.

  4. Let it cure — no water for 2 hours

    Surface-dry in 2 hours, full crystallization in 24 hours. Keep water away for the first 2 hours — if it gets wet, wipe immediately. Skipping the car wash on day one is the smart move.

    Surface-dry 2 hFull cure 24 hNo water first 2 h
⚠ Precautions: Exterior plastic only — do not use inside the car / The liquid is flammable; store cool and dark, away from flames / Do not mix with other products / If it gets in eyes or is swallowed, rinse with clean water and see a doctor if symptoms persist.
