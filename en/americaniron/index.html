How to use

Everything on this page, every number — the key rule: different parts get different treatment. Engine goes thin, chrome gets wiped, spokes go thick

  1. Prep: strip off all dirt and oils

    Components must be clean and fully dry before coating — oil films are what stop the coating gripping the real surface

    Clean, fully dry

  2. 2–3 drops on the foam, spread evenly

    Put 2–3 drops on an applicator foam and spread across the target area, one section at a time

    2–3 drops per section

  3. Thick or thin, wipe or leave — read the part

    Engine: thin coat only, and no wipe-off — lay it on thick and the finish won't look good
    Chrome: apply, then it must be wiped off — wait at least 2 minutes, never past 5, then gently buff the excess off with a microfibre cloth in circles until it gleams. Leave no residue or you'll get streaking / past the window = extremely hard to buff out
    Spokes and parts nobody sees: go thick and don't wipe at all — this is rust protection at its absolute best

    Engine = thin, no wipeChrome = wipe at 2–5 minSpokes = thick, no wipe

  4. Initial cure: 24 hours, no water

    Let it cure at least 24 hours somewhere dry — don't leave the vehicle out in the open, and no water for the full first 24 hours

    24 hrs, no water

  5. Full cure: 7 days — full properties

    Full cure lands at 7 days, and only then do you get the complete heat resistance and hydrophobic performance

    Full system in 7 days
