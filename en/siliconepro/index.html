How to use

Everything finishes on this page, both modes — the normal 7-step method and the fiberglass mode for the god-tier durability crowd (two different recipes, don't mix them up). The heart of the job: surface prep and lid discipline.

  1. Prep the surface

    The surface must be completely dry, free of dust and grease — wash it clean and let it truly dry before you start. The film only grips as well as the surface is clean.

  2. Fill every crack and joint first — always

    Rooftop work: fill cracks and joint grooves with PatchPro only, then coat SiliconePro over — SpackleFlex belongs to metal-sheet roofs, not rooftop decks. Skip this step and the whole system fails.

  3. Primer — only when painting over tiles

    Painting over glazed tiles requires a MarineGuard primer coat first, every time — ordinary concrete: skip this step entirely, primer adds nothing. Don't spend money for free.

    Tiles = primer mandatory

  4. Stir it properly, then decant

    Stir the tub until fully uniform — no shortcuts here. Then pour out only what you'll use and close the lid tight immediately. Never leave the lid open while working.

    One component, no weighingNever leave the lid open

  5. First coat

    Brush or roller, cover everything — corners and joint lines included. An even film beats a patchy thick one. (Going fiberglass mode? See the dedicated recipe in the box below — it's a different method from normal painting.)

  6. Second coat and beyond

    Wait about 2 hours after the first coat, then repaint — two coats minimum for strength, and remember the iron law: thicker = longer. A third coat never hurt anyone.

    ≈ 2 hrs between coatsNormal mode: 1 kg ≈ 5 m² (2 coats)

  7. Let it cure before using the area

    Leave the finished film at least 6 hours before walking the area again — a little patience buys a film that stays for years.

    Reopen area ≥ 6 hrs

Fiberglass mode — god-tier durability, 8–10 years

A different recipe from normal painting, and seriously heavy on material — in this mode 1 kg may cover only about 1 m². The trade: durability you can brag about for a decade. (No MarineGuard primer needed here — the cloth reinforces the film structure by itself.)

  1. Paint a thin base coat of SiliconePro first, only along the strip where the cloth will go.
  2. Lay woven fiberglass cloth onto the wet film — press it flat and tight, no air bubbles, no wrinkles.
  3. Topcoat over it again and again until not a single white spot of cloth remains visible — see white anywhere, paint over it until it's gone.
Cloth mode: 1 kg ≈ 1 m²8–10 years of serviceNo primer needed
⚠ Cloth-mode iron law: every visible white spot = a pinhole water can slip through and pool under the cloth — and that fails harder than no waterproofing at all. Paint until the white is gone completely. Zero tolerance.
⚠ Lid discipline: once opened, best used up in one go — or decant just enough and close the lid tight immediately. Never leave it open while working, or the tub starts curing and the whole thing is wasted.
Service life — straight talk: about 5 years painted bare / 8–10 years with woven fiberglass — and either way, film thickness is your multiplier: the thicker you paint, the longer it lasts.
Painting over tiles: glazed tile must be primed with MarineGuard first, every time — paint it bare and the film won't hold. Don't risk it.
