Repair work isn't paint work — you can't divide kilograms by square meters. Consumption depends entirely on how wide and how deep each wound is. Re-fixing tiles with a thin buttering on the back goes a long way; packing a deep cavity behind a tile eats material many times faster. Iron rule #1

Mix only what you'll use — never the whole kit. Once mixed, the clock runs one way: toward hard. There's no saving leftovers. Size up the wound first, then scoop exactly what that round needs.

Iron rule #2

Mix until it's truly one uniform color, no streaks. Poorly blended two-part material is the number-one cause of "why won't it harden" — any spot where A never met B stays soft forever.

Honest scope

How to use

Everything you need is on this page — underwater work comes down to a clean wound and a properly mixed batch. Nail those two and the repair stays for good.

  1. Prep the wound — clean first

    Scrub off algae, loose mortar and debris around the damage (you can do this underwater — no need to dry anything). On smooth glossy surfaces, roughen the area with sandpaper for an extra grip advantage.

  2. Mix A : B = 2 : 1

    Scoop the two parts at A : B = 2 : 1 and knead until the color is completely uniform — no streaks. Don't rush this step: any unmixed pocket will never harden. And mix only what you'll use in this round.

    A : B = 2 : 1Mix small batches

  3. Press it in — squeeze the water out

    Tile re-fixing: butter the back of the tile and press it firmly back into position, holding it in place. Crack repair: pack the compound into the crack all the way to the bottom. Firm pressure is what drives the water out of the contact surface so the compound meets the real substrate.

  4. Let it set 4 hours — hands off

    Leave the repair to set underwater within 4 hours — no touching, no poking. That's the reaction doing its job. Testing it early with your finger is how good repairs get ruined.

    Sets underwater in 4 h

  5. Swim on

    Once set, the pool goes straight back into service — no refilling, no rebalancing chemistry for the whole pool, because you never drained it in the first place.

⚠ Safety: Wear chemical-resistant gloves and eye protection while mixing and applying / for above-water work, keep the area ventilated / keep out of reach of children and pets.
