STRAIGHT TALK — READ THIS BEFORE YOU PANIC

Seeing a rainbow sheen on your panels?
That means it is working — not a defect

4.4%AVG. LIGHT TRANSMITTANCE INCREASE · 4 MONTHS OUTDOORS

Plenty of people coat their panels, then spot a faint rainbow sheen (iridescent effect) when sunlight catches the glass — and worry it is blocking light or hurting the panel. The answer: output does not drop. It actually goes up.

That rainbow is not grime or leftover solvent — it is thin-film interference, nano-scale optics: the same principle as the multi-coat treatment on premium eyeglass and camera lenses. Bare panel glass naturally reflects away around 4–5% of sunlight; our ultra-thin film cuts that reflection and pulls more light through to the cells underneath.

Real lab measurements

Measured with a spectrophotometer: average light transmittance up 2.5% immediately after coating, rising to 4.4% after 4 months of real outdoor use.

Power output measured too

Beyond light readings, we measured actual power output of coated vs uncoated panels — coated panels produced up to 2% more electricity.

How to use

Everything you need, right on this page — and the one rule that matters most: apply the thinnest coat you can, one pass only.

  1. Clean the glass first — always

    Wipe off all dust, oil and old residue before coating — the surface must be bone dry for the coating to bond fully.

  2. One thin pass — never scrub back and forth

    Use a T-shaped rubber window squeegee with a clean cloth over the rubber edge, and pour the liquid straight onto the cloth — never spray the panel directly, and never use a sponge (it drinks the liquid and wastes a lot). Then draw it across in even lanes for a thin, uniform film across the glass — the thinner the better. Scrubbing back and forth will wreck the finish and burn product for nothing.

    Squeegee + clothPour onto cloth, never sprayOne pass only100g ≈ 16 m²

  3. Let it set

    Let the film dry and bond — watch the pacing in the demo clips below. No re-wiping or buffing afterwards.

  4. Not convinced? Test before you install

    Coat a spare piece of glass first, then try wiping the rainbow sheen off with a regular alcohol-free cleaner — you will instantly understand why this lasts ~5 years.

  5. Let nature take over

    Next time wind or rain hits, dust washes off far more easily — dramatically fewer cleaning trips. That said, between rains settled dust still trims output a little, as on any panel — this is not a 100% dust force-field.

  6. Service life ~5 years

    When it is near end-of-life, or dust starts clinging noticeably easier again, simply re-coat to keep the performance.

    Service life ~5 years
⚠ Never scrub back and forth: repeated passes leave a thick, uneven film, waste product, and risk streaking — thinnest possible, one pass, done.
Save on cleaning crews: this coating clearly cuts how often panels need washing — but between washes, settled dust still costs a little output naturally. It is a maintenance-reducer, not a 100% dust shield.
